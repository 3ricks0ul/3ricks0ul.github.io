Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers every app listed on the home page. They are personal, self-hosted apps: I run them on my own servers for myself and my household.

What the apps collect

  • Google account data, only when I connect my own Google account to an app: my name and email address, to show which account is connected, and the data covered by the permissions I grant on Google's consent screen (listed per app below).
  • What people enter in an app, such as a request. Household members do not need an account, and the apps do not collect their names, emails or locations.
  • Technical logs kept on the server for a short time to fix problems.

The apps use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Cookies are only used to keep an admin signed in.

How Google user data is used

Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the app's own features to me, the account owner. It is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine learning models. People do not read it, except when I look at my own data or need to fix a problem.

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Per app

  • ec-request uses Google Drive read-only access (drive.readonly) to list the shared drives available to me and the names, sizes, dates and checksums of the files on the drives I select, and to save a copy of the files I approve to my home server. It never changes, deletes or shares anything on Google Drive. To label files, it looks up their names in a public metadata service; it sends me notifications on Telegram that contain no Google data beyond a file's name.

Where data is stored, and for how long

Everything stays on the server the app runs on. Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted. Data is kept while the app needs it and deleted when the Google account is disconnected or the app is removed.

Removing access

Each app can disconnect Google from its admin page, which deletes the stored token. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect children's personal data.

Changes and contact

This page changes when an app is added or changes what it accesses; the date at the top says when. Questions: open an issue.
